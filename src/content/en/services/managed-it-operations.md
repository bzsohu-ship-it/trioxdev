## We provide not only the server, but also the people who run it.

Monitoring, helpdesk, backup, security and documentation under one roof—so you can focus on your business, not on your systems.

---

## Why outsource?

At most companies, IT is invisible until it breaks. After that, everything else stops.

Your in-house IT person goes on vacation, quits, or is putting out three fires at once. The knowledge is in their head, not documented. Nobody knows for sure whether the backup works. And costs are unpredictable: nothing for months, then a large, unexpected bill.

**With managed operations:**

- **costs are predictable**—a monthly fee, not ad hoc invoices
- **no single-person risk**—a team stands behind it, not a single colleague
- **the environment is documented**—we have the knowledge too, in writing
- **prevention, not firefighting**—we deal with a problem before you would even notice it
- **one party is accountable**—no providers pointing fingers at each other

---

## What does the service include?

### Monitoring and oversight

Continuous monitoring of servers, the network and critical services. We are the first to receive alerts, and we are the ones who handle them—you don't have to watch the system.

- server, network and service monitoring
- alert handling according to a defined procedure
- capacity and performance monitoring, capacity planning
- availability reporting

### Helpdesk and user support

Your staff can report their problems in one place and get a response within the time set in the SLA—in Hungarian, from named specialists.

- SLA-based response, with response times that vary by priority
- remote and on-site support
- device and account management for employees joining and leaving
- permission management

### Workstation and device management

- central management and inventory of devices
- patch management for the operating system and applications
- endpoint protection deployment and monitoring
- device lifecycle tracking, replacement planning

### Server and infrastructure operations

- operation of physical and virtual servers (Hyper-V, VMware, Proxmox)
- Active Directory and directory services
- file, database and application servers
- patch and update management in planned maintenance windows

### Network and security

- management of firewalls, VPNs and network devices
- network segmentation: separate internal (database, application) and public (web, mail) layers
- endpoint protection, multi-factor authentication
- security audits, identifying vulnerabilities

### Microsoft 365 and cloud

- Microsoft 365 rollout and full administration
- operating Azure environments, hybrid solutions
- license management and optimization—pay only for what you actually use
- email and anti-phishing protection

### Backup and restore

- operating enterprise-grade data backup
- multiple independent physical backup targets, at multiple sites
- off-site VM replication for critical workloads
- regular restore tests—a backup is only worth something if it can actually be restored

*Related service: [Audit Restore Validation and restore testing](/en/services/audit-restore-validation/)*

### Documentation and reporting

- regular, automated infrastructure audits
- up-to-date environment documentation, which also belongs to you
- client reports: a resource report filtered to your own environment (hosts, VMs, network)
- change tracking

### Consulting

- IT strategy and development plan, IT leadership support
- procurement advice, purchasing devices and licenses
- planning and delivering migration and modernization projects
- cost optimization

## How do we get started?

### 1. Assessment

We map your existing environment: servers, network, workstations, licenses, backup, risks. This is the basis of the quote—with concrete numbers, not estimates.

### 2. Quote and service level

A package and SLA tailored to your needs. No rigid packages you have to fit into: you pay for what you actually need.

### 3. Takeover and documentation

We take over the environment, document it, and put the monitoring and management tools in place. If needed, we coordinate with the previous provider or the departing colleague for an orderly handover.

### 4. Stabilization

In the first weeks, we fix the gaps found during the assessment: missing updates, backup gaps, security holes, outdated permissions.

### 5. Ongoing operations and reporting

From here on, it runs on its own. You receive regular reports on what happened in your environment and what is worth preparing for in the coming period.
